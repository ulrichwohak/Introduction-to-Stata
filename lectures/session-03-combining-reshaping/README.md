# Lecture 3: Combining hotel data

Teaching date: 5 October 2026, before the separate Lecture 4 on regressions.

Assessment: checkpoint 2 of 4, worth 10% of the overall course grade.

## Learning goals

- Distinguish hotel attributes from repeated price quotes and identify their keys.
- Append the 2017 and 2018 price files.
- Merge hotel attributes into repeated quotes and inspect `_merge`.
- Validate the combined key, construct nightly prices, and save the full panel.

There are no regressions in this lecture or its required exercise. Reshaping and
aggregation are optional extensions in `optional-reshaping.do`.

## Lecture and exercise

Run `lecture.do` from the repository root after the prepared data have been supplied
or generated by `scripts/01_prepare_data.do`. It needs `hotel_prices_2017.dta`,
`hotel_prices_2018.dta`, and `hotel_features.dta` under `data/derived/`.
It saves the complete combined data as `session03_hotel_panel.dta`.

The exercise reverses the merge direction: start with one row per hotel and merge
in only the 2018 prices. Choose the cardinality from the keys, inspect unmatched
hotels, keep matched quotes, validate the combined key, and calculate nightly prices.
Consult `help merge` and `help isid`.

Submit your completed `exercise.do` and `output/logs/session03_exercise.log`.
The starter is runnable but contains TODO prompts, not completed answers.
No regression, CSV export, or reshaping is required for this checkpoint.

The [session plan](../../schedule/session_plan.md) allocates this material to the
first part of 5 October. Lecture 4 begins afterwards in a separate do-file.

## Optional reshaping

Run `optional-reshaping.do` after this lecture to explore long/wide layouts and
city/year aggregation. It explains how reshaping may create explicit missing rows
for hotel/search combinations without observed quotes. The exercise offers an optional
city/month variation. Neither extension is required for checkpoint 2 or the exam.
